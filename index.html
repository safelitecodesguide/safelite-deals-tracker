Why a board instead of a list?

A coupon list tells you a code exists. A board tells you whether it works right now. Every deal here carries its last check date, the sample result it produced, and a status. When a deal dies in a re-check, it leaves this board and goes to the dead-deals board — it is never quietly left here to waste your time.

Common deal questions

What is the biggest Safelite deal available now?

ADDS100 at $100 off replacement — the largest saving tracked on October 21, 2026. For chip repair, SAVENOW2026 at $25 off is the top deal.

Can I stack two codes for a bigger saving?

No. Safelite checkout accepts one code per order, which is why the board ranks deals individually instead of suggesting combinations.

Will these deals work with an insurance claim?

No — Safelite's offer terms exclude insurance claims (and commercial/fleet work). The deals on this board apply when you pay on your own and schedule on safelite.com.

Is 10THANKYOU10 really 10% off?

Not of the full total. In checkout testing it removed $39.08 from a $787.63 quote (about 5%, subtotal-based). It is Safelite's group code for military, AAA, AARP and first responders; eligibility is checked at the appointment.

How fresh is this board?

It reflects the October 21, 2026 re-check and is refreshed every two days. A deal's status can change between checks, so always confirm the discount in your own quote.
