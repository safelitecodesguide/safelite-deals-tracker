About this tracker

The Safelite Deals Tracker is an independent deal-status board. It exists because coupon lists rot: codes die, nobody updates the list, and shoppers waste quotes on dead codes. The board's job is to say which Safelite deals are live now, backed by checkout tests dated October 21, 2026 and re-run every two days.

What it is not

  • Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Safelite AutoGlass.
  • Not a coupon feed — dead codes are published on the dead board instead of being hidden.
  • Not a guarantee: a deal live on October 21, 2026 can die tomorrow. Your own quote's discount line is the final word.
